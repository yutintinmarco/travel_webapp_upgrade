v7.3.8 Phase 2B · Background Veil transition experiment

• Replaced whole-page opacity fading with a permanent Background Veil layer.
• Glass pages never change opacity, transform or scale during profile navigation.
• Only one profile page is visible at any moment; destination DOM stays hidden until the opaque midpoint.
• At 50% progress the page swap and scroll/height changes happen behind the fully opaque background veil.
• The second half clears the veil to reveal the destination page.
• Added a 7 px edge-swipe dead zone so tiny finger movement produces no visual change.
• Removed the transition-time scroll-shell overflow mutation that could force iOS to rebuild backdrop-filter layers.
• Bottom Bar remains body-level at z-index 2000, above the veil at z-index 1500.
• No clone, snapshot, parent opacity, translate or scale is used.
