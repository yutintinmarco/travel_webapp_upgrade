v7.3.11 Phase 2B · Opaque Clip Wipe transition

Transition experiment based on the new direction:

1. No page opacity animation.
2. No CSS mask / feather-mask on the glass page.
3. No clone, snapshot, background veil or scaling.
4. Profile pages keep a stable clip-path compositing state even while resting.
5. Back gesture erases the current page from left to right using clip-path.
6. At the fully hidden midpoint, the real DOM page is swapped.
7. The previous page is then revealed from left to right. Forward navigation mirrors this direction.
8. Remaining visible pixels stay 100% opaque, so glass cards should not become semi-transparent.
9. Global background, trip header/title, page version and Bottom Bar remain untouched.
10. Gesture retains an 8 px dead zone and is close to 1:1 with the finger.

This build is intentionally focused on validating the opaque wipe behaviour before adding any decorative edge feather.
